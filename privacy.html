Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Pawar Rental Machineries ("we", "us"), Nanded City, Pune, Maharashtra, India, rents construction machines and tools. This policy explains what personal data we collect through rental.pawarhardware.com and how we use it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Quote form: your name, mobile number, the machines you need and, if you choose, your site location.
  • Calls, WhatsApp and email: whatever you share when you contact us. WhatsApp is run by Meta and has its own privacy policy.
  • Analytics (only if you accept cookies): pages viewed, device and approximate location, collected by Google Analytics.

Why we use it

We use your details only to answer your enquiry, confirm availability and rates, arrange delivery and run a rental you ask for. We do not sell your data and we do not send marketing unless you ask for it.

Cookies and analytics

The site works without cookies. Analytics cookies are off by default and load only after you press Accept in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. To remove cookies already set, clear this site's data in your browser.

Who receives your data

Our form provider (for example Formspree) delivers quote requests to us. Google receives analytics data if you accept cookies. Meta receives messages you send on WhatsApp. These providers act for us or for you and may process data outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 12 months, or longer if needed for an active rental, accounts or the law. Then we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing pawarhardware86@gmail.com. We reply within a reasonable time. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This site is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and we limit who can see enquiries. No online service is completely secure.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page with a new date. Questions: pawarhardware86@gmail.com or call 8608810589.
